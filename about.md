# About This Archive

**Classification:** Public Evidentiary Record  
**Status:** Static Archive  
**Scope:** FY 2025–26 through FY 2026–27

---

## What This Is

This website is a public, static archive of a complete forensic and
legislative record concerning cannabis prohibition, cross-border
revenue loss, and public-safety exposure in the Commonwealth of
Pennsylvania.

The materials published here were originally transmitted to members of
the Pennsylvania Senate and associated staff during the FY 2025–26 and
FY 2026–27 budget cycles.

They are reproduced here without modification.

---

## What This Is Not

This archive is not a blog.  
It is not a campaign website.  
It is not a policy advocacy platform.

No content is revised for persuasion, tone, or messaging.

---

## Publication Method

Documents are published as issued, with original dates, classifications,
and scope preserved.

Later phases may update figures or status but do not alter the content
of earlier filings unless explicitly stated.

GitHub’s version history functions as the public audit trail.

---

## Maintenance

This archive is intentionally static.

Files are added only when new phases are formally released. Existing
files are not edited except to correct non-substantive formatting errors.

---

## Contact

This archive is published for public access to the record.

No correspondence is solicited.
