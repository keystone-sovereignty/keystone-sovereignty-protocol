# The Keystone Sovereignty Protocol

**Classification:** Public Evidentiary Archive  
**Status:** Active Record  
**Initial Filing:** February 17, 2026  
**Maintainer:** Daniel Hughes, Constituent (PA-29)

---

## Purpose

This website is a public, static archive of a complete forensic fiscal
and legislative record concerning cannabis prohibition, cross-border
revenue loss, and public-safety exposure in the Commonwealth of
Pennsylvania.

The materials contained here were originally transmitted to members of
the Pennsylvania Senate during the FY 2025–26 and FY 2026–27 budget
cycles. They are published here without modification to ensure
permanent public access to the record.

This archive does not advocate.  
It does not summarize.  
It does not editorialize.

It preserves filings as issued.

---

## Structure of the Record

The Keystone Sovereignty Protocol is organized as a sequential audit,
released in discrete phases.

Each phase represents a fixed filing with a defined date, scope, and
classification. Earlier phases are not revised retroactively. Later
phases may update figures or status but do not alter the underlying
record unless explicitly stated.

The phases are published incrementally to preserve procedural
integrity.

---

## Phases

- **Phase I** — Initial Forensic Audit (Filed February 17, 2026)  
- **Phase II** — Accumulation & Liability Update  
- **Phase III** — Forensic Casualty Report  
- **Phase IV / IV-A** — Fiscal Drift & Appropriations Alignment  
- **Phase V** — Terminal Procedural Window (The Clerk)  
- **Phase VI** — Classification (reserved)  
- **Phase VII** — Post-Mortem Fiscal Attribution  
- **Phase VIII** — Challenger’s Dossier (Open Source Accountability)

Only released phases are accessible. Unreleased phases remain sealed
until published.

---

## Methodological Note

All fiscal figures are derived mechanically from documented daily loss
rates and calendar-day counts. No estimates are rounded in the
Commonwealth’s favor. No projections are substituted for closed
ledgers.

Where public-health impacts are referenced, proportional calculations
are derived from peer-reviewed sources cited in the original filings.
